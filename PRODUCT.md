# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS. User chose it explicitly because they want rich, living animation.

## Users

- **Konsumen rumahan**: buy a jar/bottle of ready-made rujak dressing to make rujak at home, for family gatherings, arisan, and snacking.
- **Penjual rujak/buah**: rujak vendors, fruit carts, and small cafes that need a consistent ready dressing in larger quantities.
- **Pembeli oleh-oleh / wisatawan**: buy it as a regional souvenir (buah tangan khas Jawa Timur).
- Reseller/partner audience is served by the Kemitraan section (the user wants it on the page).

## Product Purpose

Brand "Bumbu Rujak Manis Cak Mimin" (short: **Cak Mimin**) is an Indonesian F&B UMKM selling ready-made sweet rujak dressing (bumbu rujak manis). The landing page exists to tell the brand's story, convey the experience and the taste, and convert visitors to order (WhatsApp) or to apply for partnership.

## Positioning

- Made with **real gula aren** (palm sugar), not white sugar or artificial sweeteners.
- **Tanpa pengawet** (no preservatives).

## Operating Context

Orders are placed via WhatsApp (Indonesian UMKM norm). Rujak is eaten with fresh tropical fruit (mangga muda, bengkuang, jambu air, nanas, timun, kedondong, ubi). The bumbu is traditionally pounded on a cobek with an ulekan.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single landing page, Indonesian copy.
- **Open / placeholder (user said "pakai placeholder dulu")**: WhatsApp number, city (assumed Surabaya because "Cak" is an East Javanese address), founder story, variant names, prices, partnership terms. All placeholder values must be centralized in one config file so the owner can replace them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Bumbu Rujak Manis Cak Mimin; may be shortened to "Cak Mimin".
- Brand puts story, experience, and taste first. Must feel authentically Indonesian, characterful, cultured.
- The project folder name "rujak-nailong" is not the brand; do not use the Nailong cartoon character or its imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

No real photos, testimonials, sales numbers, certifications (PIRT/Halal), or press exist yet. Testimonials and story copy are placeholders and must be clearly marked as such in code comments/config; do not invent certifications or statistics presented as fact.

## Product Principles

1. Story before sale: the first reason to buy is who made it and how.
2. Taste is sensory: show the bumbu, the fruit, the hands; let visitors almost taste it.
3. Ordering is one tap away (WhatsApp) at every point of decision.
4. Honest ingredients: gula aren asli, tanpa pengawet, said plainly.
