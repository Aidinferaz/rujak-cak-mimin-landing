# Design System: Cak Mimin · Bumbu Rujak Manis

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Dari Nira ke Cobek"**

The page is cooked like gula aren. One molten ribbon of palm sugar pours from the top edge of the hero into a stone cobek, and the scroll follows that same thread through four stages (Nira, Wajan, Cobek, Piring) down a margin rail. Every surface sits on one textured aren-brown ground; light arrives only where the sugar catches it, as caramel. The world is warm, dark and editorial: big high-contrast serif headlines, real food photography held in a fine caramel frame, the brand's real bottles standing in pools of light, hairline rules instead of boxes.

Density is low and the rhythm is long but measured against the shorter screen side, so a laptop is not padded like a large monitor. Sections are not bands: they follow each other on the same ground, divided by a hairline held inside the gutter. Section heads split into two columns, the title on the left and the lead (and certifications) on the right. Green exists, but it is banana leaf seen in shadow: muted toward aren and reserved for the act of ordering. The identity is taken from the brand's own packaging: the script "Cm" monogram and the real bottles.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One ground: deep aren brown with a faint woven texture tile under every section, never cream, never white, never a flat band.
- Sections separate by a hairline inset to the gutter, not by a tonal step.
- Caramel is the only light: italic emphasis, numerals, the active rail stage, heat dots, the photo frame, the pool under each bottle.
- Muted leaf green appears only on action buttons.
- The brand's real packaging is the product image: cut-out bottles told apart by cap colour, fading at their foot into the ground.
- The stage rail (01 to 04) is the page's spine: in the hero, then pinned through the story, filling with a caramel thread as you scroll.

## Colors

A single warm brown family lit by caramel, with one muted leaf green held back for action.

### Primary
- **Molten Caramel** (karamel): the sugar where light hits it. Active stage dot and its halo, the story rail's filling thread, the left rule on certification items, text selection.
- **Soft Caramel** (karamel-soft): italic emphasis words inside serif headlines, the "Cm" monogram, step numerals, quote marks, filled heat dots, link and nav hover, focus outline, the kawung pattern stroke. At 35% (frame-line) it draws the photo frame; at 55% (dot-ring) it rings empty heat dots; at 22% it is the pool of light under each bottle.

### Secondary
- **Muted Banana Leaf** (daun-redam, a mix of daun toward aren-900): the fill of the order and partnership buttons, with a daun-terang mix as its border. Hover deepens toward daun-redam-hover. Raw daun and daun-terang are mixing sources, never painted directly.

### Neutral
- **Gula Aren** (aren-800): the one ground for the whole page, under a repeating texture tile (`/plates/ground.png`, 520 x 400px).
- **Gula Aren Gosong** (aren-900): the deepest step, never a section ground. Story media well behind photos, mini bar (at 82% with blur), selection text, and the mixing base for green.
- **Santan** (santan): primary text, headlines, logo name, nav.
- **Santan Redup** (santan-dim): body copy under headlines, labels, captions, logo sub-line, footer meta, completed rail stages.
- **Rail Muted** (rail-muted): the inactive stage-rail colour, a dusty brown-pink that sits back from santan-dim.
- **Hairline** (hair, santan at 18%): every divider, section separator, list rule and mini-bar border.
- **Mask Ink** (mask, #000): alpha only, inside mask-image gradients that dissolve the pour, cobek, pattern and bottles into the ground. Never painted as a colour.

### Named Rules
**The One Ground Rule.** Every section sits on the same textured aren-800 ground. No cream, paper, white or darker flat band, ever; sections separate by a hairline inset to the gutter.

**The Leaf In Shadow Rule.** Green appears only on action buttons, and always mixed toward aren-900 (62% daun at rest). Never a saturated green fill, never green text, never a green section.

**The Caramel Is Light Rule.** Caramel marks where attention lands (emphasis, active state, numerals, frames, the pool under a bottle). It is never a background area.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Taviraj (with Georgia, serif)
**Monogram Font:** Pinyon Script (the "Cm" monogram only)
**Body Font:** Hanken Grotesk (with system-ui, sans-serif)

**Character:** A heavy, high-contrast Thai-Latin serif with sharp wedges does the shouting; a sturdy, plain grotesk does the explaining. Pinyon Script carries the copperplate "Cm" from the real packaging label and is used for nothing else.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, scaled from the 1536 x 1024 hero frame, ~101px, 0.86): the hero headline only, one line per span. Below 900px or portrait it reflows at display-mobile.
- **Hero Sub** (500, 30 comp px, 1.17): the two-line hero sub; hero-sub-mobile when stacked.
- **Headline** (700, 0.98): section titles, max 17ch (22ch for the story intro), balanced wrap. The closing title runs larger (headline-closing). The last phrase may turn italic 500 karamel-soft.
- **Title** (700, 1.02): story stage headings. Bottle and companion names step down to title-product; step headings to title-sm.
- **Quote** (italic 500, 1.35): testimonials, opened by a 64px karamel-soft quotation mark (quote-mark).
- **Numeral** (italic 500, karamel-soft, tabular): step numbers 01 to 04. Prices use price (700, 24px, tabular).
- **Logo** (Taviraj 600, letter-spacing 0.012em): sized by its container (logo-hero in the hero, logo-mobile when the hero stacks, logo-mini in the mini bar, logo-footer); the monogram and sub-line scale from it in em.
- **Body Story** (400, 1.65, santan-dim): story copy, max 38ch.
- **Body Lead** (400, 1.6, santan-dim): section leads, max 46ch.
- **Body** (400, 16 to 18px, 1.6): step copy at body-lg (18px, max 44ch), button and companion copy at body (17px), bottle notes and tier copy at body-sm (16px).
- **Small text**: tier names at item-title (19px 700), nav and quote roles and certification names at nav (15px), footer meta at meta (14px), certification numbers at caption (13px, tabular).
- **Label** (700, 12px, 0.18em, uppercase, santan-dim): the heat-scale label inside the bottle data row.

### Named Rules
**The Italic Turn Rule.** A headline's closing phrase may turn italic 500 in karamel-soft ("empat tingkat pedas.", "tanpa ngulek."). One turn per headline, at the end.

**The Tabular Spec Rule.** Every number that is compared or counted (stage numbers, step numbers, prices, certification numbers, WhatsApp number) uses tabular lining numerals.

**The Packaging Script Rule.** Script type exists only as the "Cm" monogram taken from the real label. It is never used for headlines, labels or decoration.

## Layout

The hero is a fixed composition measured from the approved 1536 x 1024 comp: every position is a percentage of the frame and every size is a multiple of one comp pixel (`--k`), so the first viewport scales as one picture. Below it, the page runs on a gutter and a vertical rhythm (section-y) that takes the smaller of 10vw and 15svh, so laptop heights are not over-padded. Every section begins with a hairline from gutter to gutter.

Section heads are a two-column grid (1.35fr / 1fr, column gap head-column-gap) aligned to the gutter: the title fills the left, bottom-aligned; the lead and the certification list sit in the right column. A sample-data marker, when shown, sits above the title.

Story pins for four viewport heights: the rail at the gutter, vertically centred; a 4:5 framed photo beside it (min(36vw, 60svh) wide); the copy column after it, max 560px. Variants are a four-column row of bottles, then the Bangkok companion as a two-column row between hairlines. Steps and partnership are two-column splits, each with a 4:5 framed photo; testimonials are three columns. Under 1100px the bottles go two per row, quotes collapse to one 560px column, section heads and splits go single-column. Under 900px or in portrait, the hero becomes a flowing stack (headline, sub, then the cobek anchored to the bottom with the pour above it), the hero nav and rail hide, and the pinned story becomes a vertical list threaded by a karamel line with dots.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth comes from photography, from masks that dissolve images into the ground (the pour fades at its foot; the cobek fades at top, bottom and right; the kawung pattern fades toward the headline and the bottom edge; each bottle fades at its foot), and from caramel light. Shadow is reserved for objects that sit in the scene, never for surfaces.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Jar drop** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 20px 26px rgb(15 4 1 / 0.45))`): the Bangkok jar standing on the brown.
- **Bottle pool** (`radial-gradient(closest-side, rgb(217 154 82 / 0.22), transparent)`): a caramel pool of light under each bottle, instead of a shadow.
- **Stage halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 calc(5 * var(--k)) rgb(192 112 31 / 0.18)`): ring around the active rail dot.

### Named Rules
**The No Card Rule.** Content never sits in raised or filled boxes. Groups are separated by hairlines (1px, hair) above and below.

## Shapes

Photography and media wells are square-cornered (0) and wear the shared photo frame: a 1px frame-line outline offset 11px, so every photo (story, steps, partnership) reads as one set of mounted prints. Round geometry is reserved for small marks: the pill (999px) for buttons, circles (50%) for rail dots and heat dots. Certifications are marked by a 2px karamel left rule, not a badge. The recurring ornament is the kawung motif: four crossed ellipses with a centre dot, drawn as a 1.4px karamel-soft line at 30% opacity, tiled on the hero's right side and faded with a radial mask from the bottom-right corner plus a linear fade at the bottom edge.

## Components

### Logo
The brand's own mark, redrawn: a copperplate "Cm" monogram (Pinyon Script, karamel-soft, 0.86em) tucked above-left of "Cak Mimin" (Taviraj 600), with an optional sub-line "BUMBU RUJAK MANIS" (Hanken 600, 0.26em, 0.24em tracking, uppercase, santan-dim) flanked by two short rules at 70%. The sub-line shows in the hero; the mini bar and footer drop it.

### Buttons
Quiet, rounded and few: the only green on the page.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 1px border.
- **Leaf (primary):** muted leaf fill (daun-redam) with a daun-terang-mix border, santan text, 17px 600, padding 18px 30px, optional 22px WhatsApp glyph or trailing line arrow at a 12px gap. Used for "Pesan via WhatsApp" in the closing and the partnership action.
- **Hover:** fill and border deepen to daun-redam-hover over 0.3s ease.
- **Focus:** 2px karamel-soft outline, 3px offset, 4px radius (global).

### Text Links
- **Style:** santan, 600, 1px underline at 6px offset, trailing 18 x 11px line arrow that nudges 4px right on hover; colour shifts to karamel-soft. Used for per-product "Pesan" links.
- The hero and mini-bar "Pesan" link uses the same underlined treatment; the hero carries no WhatsApp button.

### Navigation
- **Hero nav:** six santan links in Hanken Grotesk 500 spread across the top, hover karamel-soft. Hidden below 900px or in portrait; the logo and "Pesan" remain.
- **Mini bar:** appears after the hero, fixed 64px, aren-900 at 82% with 14px backdrop blur and a hairline bottom border, slides in over 0.5s cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1). Logo without sub-line left, 15px links, underlined "Pesan" right.

### Stage Rail (signature)
The page's spine. A 1px santan-dim line at 55% with ring dots; each stage is a tabular number over its name in 600. Inactive stages are rail-muted; the active stage turns santan and its dot fills karamel with a halo; completed stages turn santan-dim and keep a karamel ring. In the pinned story, a 2px caramel thread fills down the line in step with scroll progress.

### Product Bottle (signature)
The brand's real 250 ml / 400 gr bottle, cut out from its own photo and standing at clamp(300px, 30vw, 440px) high in a caramel pool of light, fading out at its foot. Varieties (Manis, Sedang, Pedas, Super Pedas) are told apart by cap colour. Below, a hairline, the name in title-product, the heat scale, a body-sm note, then a hairline foot with the tabular price left and a "Pesan" text link right. On hover the bottle lifts 8px over 0.6s.

### Heat Scale
One scale for every bottle: a "PEDAS" label then four 10px circles, karamel-soft when filled, a dot-ring outline when empty. Never a per-variant chart.

### Companion Row
The Bumbu Rujak Bangkok jar sits in its own two-column row between top and bottom hairlines (jar left at up to 300px with the jar drop, copy right), with the same name, note and price foot as the bottles. Never a boxed card.

### Certifications
Text items with a 2px karamel left rule: the certifier name (15px 700) over its tabular number (13px santan-dim). Sits in the right column of the section head, under the lead.

### Photo Frame
Every photo (story, steps, partnership) is square-cornered, overflow-clipped and outlined 1px frame-line at an 11px offset. Story photos wipe up through the frame (clip-path, 1.1s cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.2, 1)) and settle from 1.08 scale.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep every section on the one textured aren-800 ground and separate sections with a hairline inset to the gutter.
- **Do** mix green toward aren-900 (62% daun) and use it only for action buttons.
- **Do** fill the hero's right side with the kawung line pattern (karamel-soft at 30%, radial fade from the bottom-right, dissolving at the bottom edge).
- **Do** align section heads to the gutter: title in the left column, lead and certifications in the right.
- **Do** end a headline with one italic karamel-soft phrase when it needs emphasis.
- **Do** separate content with hairlines (hair) instead of cards.
- **Do** set compared numbers in tabular lining numerals.
- **Do** show the brand's real packaging and real monogram; light products with a caramel pool, not a box.
- **Do** frame every photo with the 1px caramel line offset 11px.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put a WhatsApp button or green pill in the hero; ordering there is the underlined "Pesan" link.
- **Don't** add a cream, paper, white or darker flat band anywhere on the page.
- **Don't** paint saturated daun or daun-terang directly, or use green for text, badges or backgrounds.
- **Don't** put content in filled or shadowed cards; shadow belongs only to objects in the scene (the jar).
- **Don't** use Pinyon Script for anything but the "Cm" monogram.
- **Don't** round photographs or media wells.
- **Don't** paint #000; it exists only as mask alpha.
