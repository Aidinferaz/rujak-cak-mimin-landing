A desktop landing page for "Cak Mimin — Bumbu Rujak Manis", an East Javanese family business selling ready-made sweet rujak dressing made with real palm sugar (gula aren asli) and no preservatives. Rendered edge to edge as a finished web page, landscape 3:2, crisp readable Indonesian text, no browser window, device or desk around it.

The visitor is a home cook or rujak seller deciding whether to order. The pitch: the sauce is already made, so you can do rujak without pounding. This comp is led by the cobek: a top-down photograph of a black volcanic-stone cobek full of glossy dark rujak sauce sits at the centre of the page like a stage, a wooden ulekan resting across it, cut fruit (green mango, bengkuang, jambu air, pineapple, cucumber) arranged around it on banana leaf. Headline: "Ngerujak tanpa ngulek." Subline: "Bumbu rujak manis Cak Mimin sudah diracik. Gula aren asli, tanpa pengawet. Tinggal cocol." Primary button: "Pesan via WhatsApp".
