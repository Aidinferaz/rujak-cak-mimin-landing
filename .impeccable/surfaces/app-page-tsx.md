# Surface brief: landing page (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Single-page landing for Cak Mimin bumbu rujak manis.

Audience: home cooks, rujak/fruit sellers, oleh-oleh buyers; partners via Kemitraan.
Action: Pesan via WhatsApp (primary, every decision point); Ajukan Kemitraan (secondary).
Proof/content: gula aren asli, tanpa pengawet. All story, variants, prices, testimonials, partner terms, WA number and city are PLACEHOLDERS centralised in one content file and flagged for replacement. No invented certifications or statistics.
Sections: Hero (pour) → Cerita (01 Nira, 02 Wajan, 03 Cobek, 04 Piring) → Varian (one shared rasa scale) → Cara Ngerujak (numbered steps, margin column) → Testimoni → Kemitraan → closing CTA + footer.

Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/comp-pour-quiet.png (quiet rendition of comp round option 2, approved by user with steer: "Saya suka yang lebih simple namun perhatikan komposisinya, agar seimbang" -> balance the empty lower-right against the heavy left in the build). Superseded: .impeccable/mocks/comp-pour.png.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is cooked like gula aren: one thick ribbon of molten palm sugar pours from the top of the page and the scroll follows it, from nira to wajan to cobek to piring. Refuses the category default of a cream hero with a product jar beside a headline and a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep gula-aren brown ground (never cream), caramel amber where light hits the sugar, banana-leaf green for actions and badges, chili red as a rare accent. Faint embossed batik kawung on the brown. Bold high-contrast serif display, sturdy sans body, tabular numerals for specs. Real food photography cut into the layout like an editorial food spread; leaf-shaped green badges.

STORY: The visitor learns the sweetness is real palm sugar, not factory sugar, follows the four-stage journey, compares variants on one rasa scale, learns how to ngerujak, reads voices, and taps WhatsApp or applies to partner.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark "Cak Mimin / Bumbu Rujak Manis" top left, nav center-right, green WhatsApp pill top right. A molten gula-aren pour runs from the top edge down the left-center into a stone cobek of rujak sauce at the bottom left; green mango and jambu air bottom right; a pouch of the product with gula aren blocks on a woven tray at right with two leaf badges (Gula Aren Asli, Tanpa Pengawet). Narrow left margin column numbers 01 Nira (active, green flag), 02 Wajan, 03 Cobek, 04 Piring. Headline "Manisnya dari nira, bukan dari pabrik." in four huge serif lines at center, subline, then green "Pesan via WhatsApp" button.

FORM: Grounded candidate 7 of 7 ("Dari Nira ke Cobek", gula aren process), composition option 2 of the comp round; seed key 39631957. Signature interaction: the pour is a living element that streams continuously and, on scroll, the margin stage rail advances 01→04 as the visitor travels the journey. Raises: one shared rasa scale (VU), numbered margin steps (orizuru), single axial journey (Versailles), fixed palette with no decorative gradients (toyism), tabular spec numerals (ikeda).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
